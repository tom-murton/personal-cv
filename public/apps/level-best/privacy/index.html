Level Best: Bleep Test Trainer — Privacy Policy

Updated 29 September 2026 — optional advertising measurement and automatic interaction capture apply from version 1.4.3 (build 36), currently being prepared

The short version

Your scores, goals, profile details and runner names stay on your device. Product analytics and Apple's aggregate install attribution are enabled by default from build 33, with separate controls under Settings → Privacy. You can turn either off without limiting the app, and an existing opt-out stays off. The upcoming version also offers separate advertising measurement, only with your permission. Build 32 was prepared but never distributed.

Analytics and attribution

Level Best sends limited product-usage events to PostHog, our analytics processor. These events tell us things such as whether onboarding was completed, a run was started or finished, a result was saved, a paywall was viewed, and whether a purchase or restore flow succeeded, which app screens and features were opened, and whether audio or saving failed. They can also include the app version and build, operating-system version, general device type, a random app-install identifier and broad product state such as free or Pro access. The random identifier links actions across visits on this installation to measure journeys and returning use. This is pseudonymous usage data, not a named account. We also receive crash types and technical stack information to diagnose faults. Interaction breadcrumbs are excluded from build 33; version 1.4.3 strengthens filtering of free-text error messages in crash reports.

From version 1.4.3, PostHog also automatically measures app launches, updates, backgrounding and supported interactions with controls, including repeated taps. Interaction events contain the type of action, structural control types and identifiers assigned by the app developer. They exclude displayed text, text you type, selected values and touch coordinates. This is event measurement, not a screen recording, and it follows the same product analytics setting.

When install attribution is enabled, we also ask Apple's privacy-preserving attribution system to register three conversion milestones: install, completed first-run onboarding and a successful purchase. These values contain no purchase amount or product, fitness result, profile detail, advertising identifier or Level Best installation identifier. Apple can send delayed aggregate campaign reports to a participating advertising network, which may forward them to AppsFlyer so we can assess marketing. Builds 33–35 have no AppsFlyer SDK and send no data directly to AppsFlyer. The upcoming build 36 adds the separately permitted advertising flow below. It does not add an automatic Apple postback copy or change who controls these native conversion values.

Released builds before build 33 may instead send install and app-launch information directly to AppsFlyer through its strict SDK. Those builds send no scores, profile information, purchase events or other in-app product events to AppsFlyer; collection of Apple's advertising identifier and identifier for vendors is disabled.

PostHog product analytics events never include your score, exercise time, distance, exact goals, test dates, age, sex, runner names, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment details. We do not create an analytics person profile for you, associate your PostHog identifier with your name, use session replay or record screen contents. We do not use PostHog for tracking across other apps or websites or join its identifier to advertising data. PostHog stores this data in its EU service, and Level Best instructs PostHog not to use your IP address for location enrichment. This does not mean that network providers never process connection information to deliver or secure requests.

You can turn product analytics or install attribution off at any time under Settings → Privacy. Either choice can be changed later and does not limit the app. Existing declines are preserved; the default-on setting is not treated as an affirmative choice. Turning product analytics off stops new app-usage and diagnostic data being sent. Turning install attribution off stops future conversion-value updates, but an Apple postback already registered may still be delivered. Data already sent or an in-flight request is not recalled.

We rely on our legitimate interests in improving Level Best and understanding its marketing as the data-protection basis for the limited product analytics and native aggregate measurement above where that basis applies. The separate advertising flow below relies on your consent. This does not claim that product analytics or attribution qualifies for a consent exemption under every country's separate device-storage or access rules.

Optional advertising measurement from version 1.4.3

With your permission, Level Best uses AppsFlyer to share your advertising identifier, app opens, completed onboarding and successful purchase events with Meta and TikTok to measure and improve Level Best's ads. AppsFlyer also receives an installation identifier, app and device information, and connection information such as your IP address. This can link activity in Level Best with advertising activity on other apps or websites. We do not send your fitness results, goals, profile details, runner names, contact information, PostHog identifier, purchase amount or payment details through this flow.

The advertising SDK is enabled only after a new advertising permission and Apple's tracking authorisation. A new Allow response to our clearly described Apple tracking request records that choice. Earlier aggregate settings or an existing Apple permission alone do not grant this new use. We record the choice, notice version and date on your device. If you previously declined install attribution, we do not automatically request this permission; you can enable advertising measurement separately in Settings.

You can withdraw this consent under Settings → Privacy, or revoke tracking permission in iOS Settings. We check Apple's permission when the app returns to the foreground and stop advertising collection when permission is withdrawn. Declining does not limit the app or turn off independently enabled product analytics or Apple's aggregate attribution. We do not backfill advertising events from periods when this permission was absent. Withdrawal does not recall information already sent or an in-flight request.

Tom Murton is responsible for Level Best's use of this information. AppsFlyer processes advertising measurement for us; Meta and TikTok receive the permitted events for advertising measurement and optimisation under their own privacy terms. Their privacy information is linked below.

What stays on your device

Your test scores, training history, goals, settings and any squad or runner names you enter are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. When you share a score card or export squad results, the app hands that file to iOS so you can choose where it goes; the app itself transmits nothing.

Optional app feedback

The feedback form is available from Home, Settings, results and Support on the Level Best Pro screen. It opens in the app without an account or email requirement. Drafts stay on this device when you close the form or a send fails, until you send successfully or choose Discard draft. Nothing is submitted until you press Send.

Sending transfers your message, category, entry point, app version and build, operating-system version and locale to our shared feedback service in Neon, hosted in London. Please leave out personal information, fitness results and runner names. The app does not attach these, an account identifier, an analytics installation identifier or an advertising identifier. A random reference identifies this submission for safe retries; it is not a user identifier. Neon processes the network request and a short-lived anonymous access token. The feedback records do not store tokens or IP addresses.

We use feedback to understand problems and improve the app, and retain it until deleted by Tom Murton. Provider backups may retain deleted records within their restore period. Contact mail@tommurton.com about deletion, giving enough details to locate your message. This form has no individual reply channel. Support and feedback are reviewed in the same database.

When product analytics is enabled, we measure feedback entry points, offers, dismissals and send outcomes, and store-review request attempts. These events never contain the feedback message. Feedback submission works independently of the product analytics setting. Prompt timing and dismissal preferences are saved on this device; declining an offer does not prevent future manual feedback or determine eligibility for a later store-review request.

Purchases

Level Best Pro is a one-off in-app purchase. Payment and purchase entitlement are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. If analytics is enabled, we may receive only the product identifier and the outcome of a purchase or restore attempt. If install attribution is enabled, a successful purchase can also advance Apple's aggregate conversion value without identifying the product or amount. In version 1.4.3, if you separately permit advertising measurement, a successful purchase also sends a purchase event through AppsFlyer for advertising measurement, without its amount or payment details.

Children

Level Best does not knowingly collect names, contact details or performance data from children. Product analytics uses the same limited, pseudonymous event set for every user and can be turned off on the device.

Changes

We will update this policy before a future app update materially changes these practices.

Measurement providers

Our providers’ privacy information is available from Neon, PostHog, AppsFlyer, Meta and TikTok.
