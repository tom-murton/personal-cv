What it is

A calm sheepdog game played with one finger, in portrait. Hold a finger on the field and a faint collie shows where the dog will land; let go and he runs there and lies down facing the sheep, and the flock walks off him. Drag to draw a line and run him round. Tap a gate to swing it — shutting the pen gate is what completes a pen. The badge in the bottom corner is the bark.

Every course is a job: bring them and pen them, keep them off the corn, sort the marked from the plain up the race, gather singles off a big field, cross the lane between tractors, or bring one ewe out on her own. Each job has a par, the fewest commands a good handler would need. Three stars at par, and a clean mark when nothing broke, nobody was caught and nobody got in.

Common questions

Where are the settings?

Tap the wooden sign at the top of the field. That opens the menu, which holds every course with its best result, the settings and your record of trials. The settings cover a left-hand bark badge, reduced motion, sound, haptics and a longer judge's clock.

What is free, and what costs money?

The In-bye is free: eight jobs on the home fields, with no clock, and nothing that can be lost. The Show and the High Fell are one permanent purchase of £3.99 that unlocks fourteen more courses. One payment, no subscription, no adverts and nothing to top up.

How do I restore my purchase?

Tap the sign at the top of the field, then tap any Show or High Fell course. The purchase sheet opens, and “Restore a purchase” sits directly under the buy button. Restore checks the Apple Account that made the purchase, so if you bought it with a different account, sign in to that one in the App Store app and tap Restore again.

The sheep keep breaking away

A dog set too close, or moved too often, pushes the flock into a break. Set him further back, let him lie, and let the sheep settle before the next command. Par is the fewest commands a good handler would need, so the calmer run is usually the better score too.

Can I keep my trial records?

Yes. Open the menu from the sign, go to the trials sheet and tap Share to hand a CSV of every recorded trial to the iOS share sheet. The records live on your phone only until you do.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. The game works entirely offline. A connection is only needed to make or restore the purchase, which Apple handles.
