Foldlit support

Email support@weevolve.app. Include your Foldlit version (Settings > About), iPhone model, iOS version and what you were doing when the problem happened.

Common questions

Why does Foldlit ask to use the camera?

Choosing “Your face” shows a live mirror from the front camera, so Foldlit asks for permission the first time. The mirror runs on your iPhone; Foldlit never saves, uploads or analyses camera images and does not use the microphone. “An object” works without the camera.

If you decline, or the camera is unavailable, choose “Use light only”. To change access later, open iPhone Settings > Apps > Foldlit > Camera, then return to “Your face” and choose “Resume mirror”.

Why has the mirror paused?

Leaving the app, changing display or layout, another app using the camera, or a hot phone can pause the mirror. Choose “Resume mirror” when it is offered, or carry on with the light only.

How do I get the controls back while lighting?

Taps on the light do nothing, so you cannot change it by accident. Hold one finger still on the light for about a second to show the controls. VoiceOver users can use the “Show controls” action, and a keyboard's Escape key works too.

What is free, and what does Pro include?

Free: the live mirror and adjustable face light, Two fields with separate warmth, tint and brightness for each side (with Link, Swap and Copy), Light Table, and the free presets. There are no session limits.

Foldlit Pro is a one-off purchase, not a subscription. It adds extra presets, named setups saved on your phone for every mode, and Raking Light: a narrow band of light you position to reveal surface detail. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy. There are no adverts and no account.

How do I restore Pro?

Open Foldlit Settings and choose “Restore Purchases”; it is also on the Pro screen. Use the Apple Account that bought Pro, with an internet connection. Saved setups stay on the phone where you made them; restoring Pro does not copy them between devices.

How bright is the light?

Foldlit uses your screen as a close light source. Expect roughly the brightness of your screen at full white, which depends on your iPhone, its temperature and iOS limits. It is a practical light, not a calibrated studio light, and colours are approximate. Move the phone closer, or dim the room, for a stronger effect. Settings > Lighting can request maximum screen brightness while you light and restore the previous level afterwards, unless brightness changes during the session.

Does it work in every iPhone Duo pose?

Yes. Book, tabletop and flat use the inner screen; closed uses the outer screen with a single light. In Two fields, a single screen shows the selected field and keeps both sides' settings. Foldlit follows the active screen and layout rather than an exact hinge angle.

On other iPhones, Foldlit uses a single-screen layout: the mirror sits inside the face light, and Two fields shows one field at a time.

Which iOS version do I need?

iOS 27.0 or later on iPhone. The fold-aware layouts need iPhone Duo with iOS 27.1 or later.

How do I turn off usage sharing?

Open Foldlit Settings > Privacy and turn off “Share usage analytics”. The choice stays off and the app works the same. The privacy policy describes what is collected. To ask about or delete data already sent, email us with the Analytics ID shown in the same section.
