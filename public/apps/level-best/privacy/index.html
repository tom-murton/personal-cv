Level Best

A precise, offline bleep test trainer for police, fire and military fitness tests, with every official variant, real pass standards and training plans.

Privacy Policy

Effective 22 July 2026

The short version

Level Best collects no data. None.

What we collect

Nothing. Level Best has no accounts, no sign-in, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party data SDKs. The app works fully offline and never sends your data anywhere.

What stays on your device

Your test scores, training history, goals, settings and any squad or runner names you enter are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. When you share a score card or export squad results, the app hands that file to iOS so you can choose where it goes; the app itself transmits nothing.

Purchases

Level Best is a one-off paid app. Payment is handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

Level Best collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If a future update ever changes any of this, this policy will be updated before that update ships, and the change will be called out in the App Store release notes.
