How these charts are generated

These charts come from the same tested protocol engine that runs inside Level Best. The 20 m table totals 247 shuttles over 4,940 m; the 15 m police table reaches 35 shuttles and 525 m at the 5.4 pass mark, matching the published College of Policing figures.
