Come Bye: Sheepdog Trials

Support for Come Bye, the one-finger sheepdog game about a year on a Lakeland hill farm.

Come Bye support

Email support@weevolve.app. Include your iPhone model, iOS version and the job or screen where you need help. You can contact the person who made the game.

What it is

Choose the day's work, handle your dog and flock, earn wages, mend the farm and prepare for each season's Show. The year introduces commands through spring and remembers your farm, working companions and results.

Hold a finger on the field to place the dog, then release to send it. Draw a line for a wider path. Sheep move away from the dog, so give them room and let them settle. Read each card's weather, clock and stakes before choosing it. The herding field is played by touch.

Common questions

Where are the settings and free Trials?

At the farm, tap the Settings gear. Choose “Play Trials” to open the curated Trials. The settings include sound, haptics, reduced motion and usage sharing. In Trials, the field sign opens the course menu; hold it to open the Courses list.

What is free, and what costs money?

The complete first spring, including its lessons and local Show, and all fifteen curated Trials are free. Trials open in order as you finish the preceding course. An adopted companion stays yours if you decline the offer and can work suitable free Trials.

One permanent purchase opens summer, autumn and winter, their Shows, the personal ending and replay of the full year. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy. There are no adverts, subscriptions, consumable purchases or purchasable currency.

How do I restore my purchase?

At the farm, open Settings and choose “Restore a purchase”. Restore is also in the Trials course menu, the Courses list and the year offer. It checks the Apple Account that bought the full year and reports the result. You do not need to complete spring or open a purchase offer first. A verified purchase or restore opens paid content without resetting your saved farm.

The sheep keep breaking away

A dog set too close, or moved too often, can push the flock into a break. Set the dog further back, use lie-down when learned and let the sheep settle before the next command. A wide flank gives the flock more room than running straight through it.

What happens if I decline the purchase?

Your spring progress, farm and adopted companion stay saved. You can play the free Trials and return to the offer later. The purchase opens the remaining seasons; it does not bypass the Trials' course progression.

Can I keep my trial records?

Your trial records stay on your phone. The trial-record sheet's Share action sends a CSV to the standard iOS share sheet, where you choose the receiving app or service.

Does it need an internet connection?

Gameplay and saved progress work offline. Buying or explicitly restoring a purchase needs a connection to Apple's App Store. Usage analytics can be sent when connected and does not block gameplay.

How do I turn off usage sharing?

Open Settings and turn off “Share anonymous usage data”. The choice stays off and gameplay is unchanged. The privacy policy describes the defined events, installation identifier and reset behaviour.
