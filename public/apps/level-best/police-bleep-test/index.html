Police bleep test: what level do you need?

The UK police test is a 15m shuttle run. Entry is level 5.4, but specialist roles ask for more. Here's every standard, from recruit to firearms.

Role15m levelNotes
Recruitment & annual test5.4The national standard for joining and for the yearly in-service test (England & Wales).
Dog handler5.7Slightly above entry for the specialist role.
Police support unit (PSU)6.3Public-order trained officers.
Authorised firearms officer (AFO)7.6The armed policing entry standard.
Armed response vehicle (ARV)9.4A significant step up for response firearms roles.
Dynamic intervention (AFO)10.5The highest routine police shuttle standard.

All of these are on the 15-metre track. See the full 15m level chart for the speeds, distances and times behind each score.
