Level Best support

Email levelbest@tommurton.com. Include your iPhone model and iOS version; the in-app “Email support” button fills these in for you.

Common questions

Restore Purchases says no purchase was found

Restore checks the Apple Account that made the purchase. If you bought Level Best Pro with a different account, sign in to that account in the App Store app and tap Restore again.

What is free?

Every built-in test, the practice tracks, your results and the standards board. Level Best Pro adds the training plan, progress charts and filters, custom tests, squads over four runners and CSV export. One payment, no subscription.

The beeps are quiet outdoors

Turn the phone's volume to maximum before you start, or use a Bluetooth speaker. The mute switch does not affect the beeps.

A phone call ended my test

The app scores you to the last completed shuttle and marks the run as interrupted. Turn on Do Not Disturb before a real test.
