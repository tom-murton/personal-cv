The pass standard is 5.4, four shuttles into level 5. That's 35 shuttles, 525 metres, in a little over three minutes. It's the national standard for recruitment and the annual in-service test in England & Wales.

Because the track is shorter, you turn more often and the test feels different from the 20m version, the turns are where the effort goes. Train on the exact 15m distance; a 20m level 5.4 is not the same as a 15m level 5.4.
