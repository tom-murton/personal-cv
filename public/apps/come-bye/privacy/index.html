Come Bye: Sheepdog Trials

A calm one-finger sheepdog game: send the dog, hold the flock, shut the gate. Twenty-two courses across three fells, played in portrait with one hand.

Privacy Policy

Effective 17 September 2026

The short version

Come Bye collects nothing. There is no analytics, no advertising, no account and no server. The game works entirely offline, and everything it remembers stays on your phone.

What the app collects

Nothing. Come Bye does not collect, transmit or receive any personal data. It contains no analytics software and no third-party software development kits of any kind. It does not ask for your name, your email address or any permission, and it never asks you to sign in.

Tracking

Come Bye does not track you. It does not use Apple's advertising identifier, it does not show advertising, and it does not follow you across other companies' apps or websites. You will never see an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask for.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, your best runs, your settings and the record of every trial you play are stored on your phone by the app itself. None of it is uploaded anywhere. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

The trials sheet has a Share button, which writes your trial records as a CSV file and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet so you can choose where it goes. That only happens when you tap Share, and the app itself transmits nothing. Once you have shared a file, where it ends up is governed by whichever app or service you sent it to.

Purchases

The Show and the High Fell are one permanent in-app purchase. Payment and entitlement are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details, your Apple Account or your name, and we receive no purchase reports that identify you. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Children

Come Bye is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any future app update changes these practices. Today there is nothing to opt out of, because there is nothing being collected.
