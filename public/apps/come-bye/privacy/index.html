Come Bye: Sheepdog Trials

Your dog, your flock and a year on a Lakeland hill farm, played in portrait with one hand. The first spring, its Show and fifteen curated Trials are free.

Privacy Policy

Effective 14 October 2026

The short version

Come Bye sends defined usage data to help us understand and improve the game. It does not send your animal names or anything you type in the game. There is no account, advertising or advertising tracking. Gameplay works offline, and you can turn usage sharing off in Settings.

What the app collects

The app sends events about jobs offered and played, their outcomes, progress through the year, lessons, Shows, repairs and purchase or restore outcomes. It also sends app lifecycle events and limited technical context, including the app and iOS versions. These are defined event names, categories, counts and durations, not a recording of the screen or your input.

Each installation has a random identifier so that we can understand a play journey and whether players return. It is not joined to your name, email address, Apple Account, advertising identifier or device vendor identifier. We use PostHog Cloud EU to store and analyse these events in the European Union, for our legitimate interests in improving Come Bye. We do not use them for advertising or sell them to data brokers.

The app does not access your device's location. Location lookup from analytics request IP addresses is disabled. Like other internet requests, an analytics request carries your IP address to the provider; it is not an in-game property or a location requested from your phone.

Your choice

Usage sharing is on by default. Open Settings and turn off “Share anonymous usage data” to stop sending it. The choice stays off, queued events are removed and the installation identifier is reset. This does not remove events already received by the analytics provider. Turning sharing off does not change gameplay or your purchase.

What stays on your phone

Your saved farm, dog and sheep names, kennel, repairs and detailed trial records are stored on your phone. The app does not upload those saved files or names. It does not send anything you type in the game, purchase receipts, transaction identifiers, payment details or screen recordings. Automatic crash capture is disabled.

If you choose to share a trial-record CSV using the iOS share sheet, you choose the receiving app or service. The file is only shared when you use that action; the receiving service's own privacy practices then apply.

Purchases

One permanent in-app purchase opens summer, autumn and winter, their Shows, the personal ending and full-year replay. Apple handles payment and restoration through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details or Apple Account. Product analytics records only bounded offer, purchase and restore outcomes, without receipts, transaction identifiers or prices.

Advertising and children

Come Bye has no advertising and does not track players across other companies' apps or websites. It is intended for adults and is not directed at children. The usage-sharing behaviour described in this policy applies to the game.

Changes and contact

This policy describes the year game with product analytics and replaces the earlier policy for the course-only build. We will update it if our data practices change.

Come Bye is made by Tom Murton in the United Kingdom. For privacy questions or requests, email mail@tommurton.com. If you contact us, we use the information you choose to send to answer your request.
