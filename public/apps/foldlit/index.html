Two ways to light

Home asks one question: what are you lighting?

Your face

A live front-camera mirror with soft light you can adjust, for make-up and getting ready.

  • Adjustable warmth and intensity
  • On a dual-screen iPhone, the mirror and the light sit in separate regions, and you can swap them
  • On a standard iPhone, the mirror sits inside a surrounding light field

An object

Aim light at a small object, a piece of film or a surface. Photograph it with another camera; Foldlit provides the light.

  • Two fields: each with its own warmth, tint and intensity. Link, swap or copy them, and fold a dual-screen iPhone to aim them
  • Light Table: an even backlight for tracing or viewing film
  • Raking Light: a narrow band of light you position, resize and aim across a surface to explore its detail

Made for your screen

Foldlit works on iPhone Duo, with fold-aware layouts that need iOS 27.1 or later, and on every iPhone running iOS 27.0 or later. Book, tabletop, flat and closed poses all work. Closed uses a compact layout on the outer display, and standard iPhones use the same compact layout.

Screen area and light output depend on your device, placement and conditions. Foldlit is an independent app and is not endorsed by Apple.

Private by design

  • The camera is used only for the live mirror. Foldlit does not take or save photos or videos, and mirror frames are never stored or sent.
  • No account and no ads.
  • Usage analytics can be switched off in Settings > Privacy.
