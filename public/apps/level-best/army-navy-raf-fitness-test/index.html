Armed forces bleep test standards (RAF, Army, Navy)

The forces do not use one common entry test. The route, distance and pass mark vary, and Royal Navy requirements change on 1 October 2026.

RouteStandardNotes
Army Officer20 m MSFTCandidates should aspire to exceed 8.7; the assessment runs to 11.6.
Army soldier2 km runThe required time depends on the chosen role.
Royal Marines Officer20 m 10.8The published pre-joining fitness assessment level.
Royal Navy candidate2.4 km track runApplies to candidates attending before 1 October 2026; times vary by age and gender.
Royal Navy candidate20 m 6.6Applies from 1 October 2026, regardless of age or gender. Diving candidates require 10.5.
RAF candidateRoute-specificThe published process includes a 2.4 km pre-joining run and an MSFT at Cranwell; standards vary by age, gender and route.
Check the date and route before you train. The Royal Navy moves from a 2.4 km track run to a 20 m MSFT on 1 October 2026. Army soldiers run 2 km, while Army officers complete the MSFT. RAF requirements are route-specific.
