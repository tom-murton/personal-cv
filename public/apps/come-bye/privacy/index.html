Come Bye: Sheepdog Trials

A calm one-finger sheepdog game about a shepherd's year on a Lakeland hill farm, played in portrait with one hand. The first spring, its Show and 15 curated Trials are free.

Privacy Policy

Effective 25 September 2026

The short version

Come Bye sends anonymous usage data about how the game is played, so we can make it better. It never sends your name, your dog's or your sheep's names, or anything you type. There is no advertising, no account and no tracking. The game works offline, and you can turn usage data off in Settings at any time.

What the app collects

Come Bye sends anonymous usage data to help us make the game better: which jobs are offered and played, how each one ended, how far through the year you have got, and when the app is opened. It is sent to PostHog, our analytics provider, which stores it in the European Union. Each install has a random identifier so we can see how play goes over time; it is not linked to your name, your Apple Account, your email or any advertising identifier. We never send your dog's name, your sheep's names or anything else you type, and we do not collect your location. We use this data only to understand and improve Come Bye, never for advertising, and we do not sell or share it.

You can turn it off at any time in Settings, "Share anonymous usage data". Nothing more is sent from that moment, and the random identifier is reset. The game does not ask for your name, your email address or any permission, and it never asks you to sign in.

Tracking

Come Bye does not track you. It does not use Apple's advertising identifier, it does not show advertising, and it does not follow you across other companies' apps or websites. You will never see an App Tracking Transparency prompt, because there is nothing to ask for.

What stays on your phone

Your progress, your best runs, your settings and the record of every trial you play are stored on your phone by the app itself. None of it is uploaded; only the anonymous usage data above leaves the phone. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it.

The trials sheet has a Share button, which writes your trial records as a CSV file and hands it to the standard iOS share sheet so you can choose where it goes. That only happens when you tap Share, and the app itself transmits nothing. Once you have shared a file, where it ends up is governed by whichever app or service you sent it to.

Purchases

One permanent in-app purchase opens summer, autumn and winter, their Shows, the personal ending and replay. Payment and entitlement are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. The app receives no payment details, and we never see your Apple Account or your name. Restoring a purchase asks Apple, not us.

Children

Come Bye is intended for adults and is not directed at children. It collects no personal data from anyone, of any age.

Changes

This policy will be updated before any future app update changes these practices. The previous version (17 September 2026) described a build that collected nothing; builds from 25 September 2026 send the anonymous usage data described above.
