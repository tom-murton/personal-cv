Each 20m shuttle is one length. The test starts at 8.0 km/h and speeds up half a km/h at most levels; the beeps get closer together as you climb. A completed level 10 is 1,880m in about 10 minutes 32 seconds.

Reading a score: "10.6" means you completed 6 shuttles of level 10 before dropping out. Your score is the last shuttle you finished in time with the beep.
