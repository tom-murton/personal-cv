Level Best: Bleep Test Trainer — Privacy Policy

Updated 16 September 2026 — regional measurement applies from build 33

The short version

Your scores, goals, profile details and runner names stay on your device. Product analytics and install attribution are optional, with separate controls in Settings → Privacy. From build 33, both are enabled by default in the United States, Australia and New Zealand unless you have already opted out. In other countries we wait for your choice: a small in-app card offers product analytics and a separate install-attribution option. Both can also be changed in Settings. If we cannot determine the country, neither starts automatically. Earlier builds enabled both by default. Build 32 was prepared with separate choices but was not released.

Analytics and attribution

Level Best sends limited product-usage events to PostHog, our analytics processor. These events tell us things such as whether onboarding was completed, a run was started or finished, a result was saved, a paywall was viewed, and whether a purchase or restore flow succeeded, which app screens and features were opened, and whether audio or saving failed. They can also include the app version and build, operating-system version, general device type, a random app-install identifier and broad product state such as free or Pro access. The random identifier links actions across visits on this installation to measure journeys and returning use. This is pseudonymous usage data, not a named account. We also receive crash types and technical stack information to diagnose faults; exception messages and interaction breadcrumbs are excluded in the prepared build 33.

AppsFlyer measures an install or app launch so we can understand which marketing activity led people to Level Best. AppsFlyer receives no Level Best scores, profile information, purchase events or other in-app product events. We use AppsFlyer’s strict SDK, disable collection of Apple’s advertising identifier and identifier for vendors, do not personalise advertising, and do not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

When install attribution is enabled, we also ask Apple's privacy-preserving attribution system to register an install. Apple can send delayed, limited campaign reports to the advertising network involved. We do not encode fitness results or purchase information in that signal, or configure automatic copies to AppsFlyer. A report already registered with Apple may be delivered after you turn attribution off. App Store campaign and sales reports are separate Apple services and are not controlled by these app settings.

Analytics events never include your score, exercise time, distance, exact goals, test dates, age, sex, runner names, contacts, advertising identifiers or payment details. We do not create an analytics person profile for you, associate your identifier with your name, use session replay, record screen contents or track you across other apps or websites. PostHog stores this data in its EU service, and Level Best instructs PostHog not to use your IP address for location enrichment. This does not mean that network providers never process connection information to deliver or secure requests.

You can turn product analytics or install attribution off at any time under Settings → Privacy. Either choice can be changed later and does not limit the app. An existing opt-out is preserved regardless of country. We rely on our legitimate interest in improving the product and assessing our marketing for default-on measurement, and consent where we ask you to enable a service. Automatically enabled settings from older builds are not treated as affirmative consent. Turning a setting off stops new app usage and attribution events; data already sent or an in-flight request is not recalled.

Country check

From build 33, the app makes a short request to our country-check service, hosted by Cloudflare, to determine the connection's country from its IP address. The request contains no analytics installation ID, fitness information or GPS location. Our endpoint returns only a country code and policy version; it does not store the request or set cookies. Cloudflare necessarily processes connection data to provide and secure the service. A VPN can affect the country detected. This check does not block use of the app. Your device language and App Store country are not used to guess location.

What stays on your device

Your test scores, training history, goals, settings and any squad or runner names you enter are stored only on your device. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it. When you share a score card or export squad results, the app hands that file to iOS so you can choose where it goes; the app itself transmits nothing.

Purchases

Level Best Pro is a one-off in-app purchase. Payment and purchase entitlement are handled by Apple through the App Store. We never see your payment details. If analytics is enabled, we may receive only the product identifier and the outcome of a purchase or restore attempt.

Children

Level Best does not knowingly collect names, contact details or performance data from children. Product analytics uses the same limited, pseudonymous event set for every user and can be turned off on the device.

Changes

We will update this policy before a future app update materially changes these practices.

Measurement providers

Our providers’ privacy information is available from PostHog and AppsFlyer.
